# Discipline: Baptised Members Living in Unbelief and Sin

1. ANNOUNCEMENT OF A TRIAL PERIOD FOR AN UNFAITHFUL BAPTISED MEMBER

Session has the sad duty to make known to you that       Full Name       of       Address      , who is a baptised member of our church, has been continually living an un-Christian life. All our warnings and admonitions have been ignored, and we had to inform him/her that unless there is evidence of repentance in the next three months, we will have to sever his/her relationship with our church.

We urge you to pray for him/her and to speak to him/her in love, reminding him/her of God's covenant promises of which he/she received the sign and seal.

2. ANNOUNCEMENT OF ERASURE OF A BAPTISED MEMBER

Session has to inform you with sorrow that       Full Name       of       Address      , has not given evidence of repentance since you were informed some months ago of his/her un-Christian life. Because of his/her refusal to live in the covenant in which he/she was included, we have to sever his/her relationship with this church.

Let us pray for him/her and admonish him/her in Christian love. And let us all be aware of the danger that we, too, may fall away from the living God by loving this present world.
