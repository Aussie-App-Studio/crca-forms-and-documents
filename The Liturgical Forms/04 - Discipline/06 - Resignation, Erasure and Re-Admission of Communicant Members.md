# Discipline: Resignation, Erasure and Re-Admission of Communicant Members

1. ANNOUNCEMENT OF RESIGNATION OF A COMMUNICANT MEMBER

With sadness, we have to announce that       Full Name      , who used to be a communicant member of this congregation, has informed us that he/she no longer wants to belong to our church.

We pray that by the grace of the Holy Spirit, by our prayers and by our loving admonition, he/she may return one day into our fellowship.

2. ANNOUNCEMENT OF ERASURE OF A COMMUNICANT MEMBER

With sadness, we have to announce that       Full Name      , who despite much encouragement has ceased for a long time now to show interest in the life of the church, will be erased from the roll of this church in        weeks' time unless a change of heart is obvious. We urge you to pray for him/her and speak to him/her.

3. ANNOUNCEMENT OF RESIGNED OR ERASED PERSON REQUESTING RE-ADMITTANCE AS A COMMUNICANT MEMBER

With gratitude and joy we inform you that       Full Name       who had resigned / was erased as a member of our church, has requested to be accepted again into full communion.

Session has granted this request and we now urge you to receive this brother/sister with love, praising God for His gracious guidance and faithfulness.
