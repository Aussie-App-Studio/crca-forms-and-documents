# Discipline:

Public Exhortations
Re Communicant Members

FIRST PUBLIC ADMONITION OF CHRISTIAN DISCIPLINE[^1]

It is the sad duty of your Session to inform you that one of our brothers/sisters, a communicant member, has for some time already been barred from the Lord's table because of his/her un-Christian life.

Despite urgent and repeated warnings by the office bearers, we found no evidence of repentance. We now have to inform you that this member of our church is in grave spiritual danger and if he/she does not turn back to the Lord, we may ultimately have to excommunicate him/her.

Making this known to you for the first time, we plead with you to remember in your prayers all the covenant members who are backsliding, and especially those in our fellowship.

May our gracious Lord and Saviour hear our prayers.

SECOND PUBLIC ADMONITION OF CHRISTIAN DISCIPLINE

As you will remember, some years/months ago, we informed you that one of our members had to be barred from the Lord's table because of his/her un-Christian life. We have prayed, we have pleaded and lovingly admonished, but all our efforts so far were in vain. This brother/sister has consistently refused to listen to the church. He/she has not yet shown repentance.

In obedience to the Word of the Lord we must now proceed. We have asked the advice of the churches in our Classis and were told that we should inform our congregation and announce the name of the brother/sister concerned.

His/her name is       Full Name       of       Address      .

We urge you to pray for him/her and, if at all possible, to visit him/her and to plead with him/her in love and compassion to return to the obedience and joy of our Lord and Saviour.[^2]

If he/she refuses to listen and to repent, your Session will need to excommunicate him/her from our church and declare that he/she can no longer be a member of God's kingdom.[^3]

THIRD PUBLIC ADMONITION OF CHRISTIAN DISCIPLINE

Session informed you some time ago that       Full Name       of       Address       is living an un-Christian life, for which reason we had to bar him/her from the Lord's table. We also told you that unless true repentance was shown we would have to proceed with Christian discipline.

We have now come to the sad conclusion that he/she has hardened his/her heart and appears to be an unbeliever.

Let us all pray fervently during this week that it may please the Lord to work mightily in the heart of       Full Name      . If we find no evidence of repentance during this week, we will have to proceed with discipline, and next Sunday exclude him/her from the fellowship of the church by public excommunication and declare that he/she can no longer be a member of the kingdom of God.

May the Lord in His great mercy hear our prayers.

---

[^1]: This first admonition can be given at the discretion of Session. For the subsequent two admonitions, which involve the naming the delinquent, the concurrence of the Classis is required.
[^2]: Galatians 6:1-2; Luke 15:7, 10; Psalm 51:12, 17
[^3]: 1 Corinthians 5:1-5, 13; 6:9-10
