# Discipline: Form for the Re-Admission of Excommunicated Persons

After the sermon, the minister shall say:

Congregation of our Lord Jesus Christ.

We have previously announced to you the gladdening news of the conversion of our brother/sister       Full Name       who has been excommunicated from the Church of Jesus Christ. We have not received any valid objection against his/her re-admission to full communion with the church. Therefore, in the name of the Lord and with great joy, we shall now proceed to do this.

Our sovereign Lord says in His Word, "I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but rather that they turn from their ways and live."[^1] Our Lord Jesus Christ, the great Shepherd, gave His life for His sheep[^2] and became the Door that they might find life and abundance.[^3] He tells of the joy in heaven even over one sinner who repents.[^4] So also Paul says,

"If anyone has caused grief, ...you ought to forgive and comfort him, so that he will not be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow. I urge you, therefore, to re-affirm your love for him." [^5]

The Lord Jesus who said that "whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven" also assured us that "whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven."[^6] We therefore were given authority to assure anyone who truly repents of sin that he should not doubt that God accepted him in grace: "If you forgive anyone his sins, they are forgiven."[^7]

Now that it may be clear to us all that you, brother/sister       Full Name       are returning to the profession of your faith and love towards your Lord in true repentance, you are requested to arise and to answer the following questions:

1. Do you declare with all your heart, here before God and His church, that you are sincerely sorry for the sin and error that had caused your estrangement from God and His church?

2. Do you also truly believe that the Lord God Himself, for the sake of Jesus Christ, has forgiven you and still forgives you all your sins, and has restored you into fellowship with Him?

3. Is it therefore your desire to be re-admitted to the Church of Jesus Christ and to full communion with this congregation? Do you promise by the grace of God to live according to His will, both in doctrine and in conduct, faithful to Jesus your Lord in life and in death?

Full Name , what is your answer?

Answer: By the grace of God, with all my heart, I do.

The whole congregation shall arise and the minister shall say:

We then, being here assembled in the name and authority of Jesus Christ, declare you,       Full Name      , to be absolved from the bonds of excommunication. We receive you again into the church of the Lord. We declare unto you that you stand in the communion of Christ, of the holy sacraments, and of all the spiritual blessings and privileges which God promises and gives to those in His church and kingdom.

May our eternal God preserve you in this to the glorious Day of His Son Jesus Christ. To Him be the glory, now and for ever.

All say: Amen!

Then, while all remain standing, the minister shall continue with the following charges:

Be assured in your heart, dear brother/sister, that the Lord Himself has received you in grace. In all your thoughts, words and actions, do not cease to praise Him for the great love He gave you.

Be watchful against the wiles of Satan and the wickedness of the world, that you may not fall again into sin. Seek earnestly the fellowship of your brothers and sisters, that you may not lack strengthening and encouragement. Love Christ, for much has been forgiven you.[^8]

To the congregation:

And you, beloved Christians, receive this brother/sister of yours with love from the heart.

"...Celebrate and be glad, because he/she was dead and is alive again; he was lost and is found." [^9]

Rejoice with the angels in heaven over this sinner who has come to repentance.[^10] Count him/her no longer as a foreigner and alien but as a fellow citizen with God's people and a member of God's household.[^11]

A psalm or hymn may be sung, followed by prayer:

Almighty God and merciful Father.

We humbly thank and praise Your name! You have graciously heard our prayers. You have given life where there was death, repentance where there was rebellion, and joy where there was grief and mourning.

We acknowledge that it was Your goodness alone which has brought back our brother/sister to the fold of Your people. It was the powerful working of Your Word and Spirit, and Your sovereign use also of frail human means. To Your name be praise and glory, O Shepherd of Your people, O God of all grace!

We now humbly ask You that You will continue Your work in the heart of this our brother/sister. May he/she walk with You all the days of his/her life, praising Your goodness and resisting the evil one, who, as You promised, will then flee from him/her.[^12]

Make him/her able, in turn, to warn and help others in the struggle against evil. So restore to him/her the joy of Your salvation, that he/she will teach transgressors Your ways, and sinners will turn back to You.[^13]

Grant him/her thus, finally, with all Your redeemed children, on the Day of Christ, to receive the crown of glory that will never fade away.[^14]

Through Him, our Lord Jesus Christ, who with You and the Holy Spirit, one only God, lives and reigns forever. Amen.

Here the service continues with the celebration of the Lord's Supper. (Form V is recommended for this occasion.)
