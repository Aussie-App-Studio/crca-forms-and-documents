# Discipline: Public Announcement for the Re-Admission of Excommunicated Persons

Beloved in the Lord.

It is known to you that some time[^2] ago our fellow member       Full Name       of       Address       was excommunicated from the Church of Jesus Christ. It is our great joy to be able to announce to you now that the Lord has heard our prayers, blessed our warnings, and led this brother/sister to repentance.

Full Name       has now requested to be re-admitted to the fellowship of the church. Session has heard his/her testimony and is satisfied that, as there had been grounds to declare this brother/sister bound because of his/her sin, now there are grounds to declare him/her released, according to the command of Christ.[^3]

We therefore intend to receive him/her again into the full communion of the church when we hope to celebrate the Lord's Supper on    Date   , unless any of you, in the meantime, should have valid reasons why this ought not to be done, of which you must give notice in due time.

Meanwhile, let everyone thank the Lord for His grace shown this brother/sister, and pray that the Lord will continue and complete His work begun in him/her.[^4]

---

[^1]: To be read at least two Sundays before the proposed date of re-admission.
[^2]: If it is helpful, the time lapsed may be mentioned here, e.g., two years ago.
[^3]: Matthew 16:19; John 20:23
[^4]: Psalm 138:8; Philippians 1:6
