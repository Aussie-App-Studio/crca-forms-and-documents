# Discipline: Form for Excommunication

Beloved in the Lord Jesus Christ.

It is known to you that, from time to time, we have made announce-ments concerning our brother/sister       Full Name      . His/her life has given us concern and we have had reason to question his/her standing in God's covenant and church. You were asked several times to pray for him/her and, in brotherly love, to urge him/her to return from his/her way of death and repent before the Lord his/her God.

However, no evidence of such repentance has been brought before us. Therefore the Session of this church, having heard the advice of the Classis, does now have the sad duty to proceed with his/her excommunication. We do this because Christ commanded it:

"...if he refuses to listen even to the church, treat him as you would a pagan...I tell you the truth, whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven."[^1]

This is also clearly taught by the apostle Paul. He warns that the wilful toleration of evil in the church dishonours God and endangers His people.[^2]Clearly he instructs the church at Corinth that the wicked person must be expelled from their midst.[^3] In Christ's name he charged the elders at Ephesus to guard the flock from those appearing to be wolves, even when they arose from their own community.[^4] All Scripture clearly teaches that discipline is to safeguard God's church from disobedience, pollution and temptation, both in doctrine and life. In this way the glory and majesty of God are to be upheld in the midst of His people.[^5]

At least as strongly, however, God's Word tells us that the loving aim of harsh measures is to drive sinners back to repentance.[^6] In God's own stern dealings with His disobedient people we always hear His pleading call to return to Him:

"As surely as I live, declares the Sovereign LORD, I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but rather that they turn from their ways and live. Turn! Turn from your evil ways! Why will you die, O house of Israel?"[^7]

Even at the point that we must declare someone excluded from the church and kingdom of God, we — on God's behalf — are to call him/her to return from his/her ways and find life.

According to the command of God, you, members of this church, are to extend this urgent call of love to him/her.[^8] However, you must not give him/her the privilege of teaching you[^9] or the opportunity to lead you. [^10]You cannot give him/her the kind of fellowship that would be a sharing of faith, because that would be compromising the truth of God's Word and give him/her a false sense of security.[^11]

Rather, we must pray fervently to our Lord, that He will bring him/her to repentance and life. We are also called to take heed that, as we think we stand firm, we shall not fall ourselves.[^12]In this we all are made aware again of our own need of the saving and preserving grace of Jesus Christ through His Holy Spirit.[^13]

Finally, as a church of Jesus Christ, we must deeply humble ourselves before the Lord our God. We mourn and grieve that the bond of love among us has not prevented our brother/sister from straying away. As we take the step of excommunication in obedience to the command of God, it is in a deep sense of our own shortcomings and weaknesses. We know that we must watch and pray so that we will not fall into temptation.[^14]

The congregation is requested to stand, after which the minister shall say:

In the name and authority of Jesus Christ, we, as servants and overseers of His church at this place, declare that we excommunicate       Full Name       from the church and declare that he/she can no longer be regarded as a member of the kingdom of God, unless he/she repents. So long as he/she persists obstinately in his/her rebellion against the revealed will of God, he/she is excluded from the fellowship of Christ, from the holy sacraments and from all the spiritual blessings and privileges which God promises and gives to His church and kingdom.

To Him be the glory. Amen.

The congregation is seated.

Prayer:

Righteous God and merciful Father.

We appear before Your high and holy majesty in fear and trembling, knowing that we, every single one of us, have grievously sinned against Your holy will. We confess that we, by our own disobedience, have made ourselves worthy of being banished from the light of Your face forever.

On this sad day for our congregation we come to You in humble repentance. O Lord, do not enter into judgment with Your servants. Receive us into Your grace only for the sake of Jesus Christ, our Saviour. We earnestly ask You, O Lord, that You will remove and keep far from our hearts the pride that exalts itself above other sinners. Only Your infinite mercy can keep our own feet on the way of life.

Lord, we plead that mercy alone, when we now pray for       Full Name       who was excommunicated today from Your church and kingdom. You Yourself commanded us to do this. You know how we have sought to restore him/her to full submission to Your will. You have heard us plead with him/her to come back to joyful obedience to Your law of gratitude. We saw no signs of this but only hardness of heart. O God, again we pray to You that You will yet change his/her heart and bring him/her back to You in repentance unto life, so that there will be the joy of feasting in the house of his/her Father.[^15]

For the sake of the blood of Jesus, O Lord, forgive us the shortcomings that have marred our work with this brother/sister. Forgive us the way in which we, as a congregation, may have contributed to his/her error. Create in our hearts a greater concern over him/her as well as others who might be in danger. Give us the grace not to be faithless in our dealings with one another.

Grant, O merciful God and Father, that, as we mourn now over this man/woman, we may one day rejoice over him/her with the very angels in heaven,[^16] through Him, Our Lord Jesus Christ, who taught us, thus to pray:

Our Father in heaven,

hallowed be Your name,

Your Kingdom come,

Your will be done, on earth as in heaven.

Give us today our daily bread.

Forgive us our sins, as we forgive those who sin against us.

Lead us not into temptation but deliver us from the evil one.

For the kingdom, the power and the glory are Yours,

now and for ever.

Amen.
