# Form For The Ordination / Installation Of Ministers

THE OPENING OF THE SERVICE[^1]

M[^2] "Comfort, comfort My people, says your God.

speak tenderly to Jerusalem and proclaim to her

that her hard service has been completed,

that her sin has been paid for,

C[^3] that she has received from the LORD's hand

double for all her sins."[^4]

M "A voice says, 'Cry out.' And I said, 'what shall I cry?'

C 'All men are like grass,

and all their glory is like the flowers of the field...

The grass withers and the flowers fall,

but the Word of our God stands for ever.'

M You who bring good tidings to Zion,

go up on a high mountain.

C You who bring good tidings to Jerusalem,

lift up your voice with a shout!

M Lift it up, do not be afraid;

say to the towns of Judah, 'Here is your God!'"[^5]

C "He tends His flock like a shepherd:

He gathers the lambs in His arms

and carries them close to His heart!"[^6]

M "You are a chosen people, a royal priesthood,

a holy nation, a people belonging to God,

C that we may declare the praises of Him who called us

out of darkness into His wonderful light!"[^7]

M Our help is in the name of the LORD

C who made heaven and earth!

M Who faithfully keeps His word

C and never forsakes the works of His hands!

M Grace unto you and peace

from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ

in the fellowship of the Holy Spirit.

C Amen!

The congregation sings (Psalm 100 or Psalm 113).

CONFESSION AND ASSURANCE

M The word of the LORD came to me:

"Son of man, speak to your countrymen and say to them: 'When I bring the sword against a land, and the people of the land choose one of their men and make him their watchman, and he sees the sword coming against the land and blows the trumpet to warn the people, then if anyone hears the trumpet but does not take warning and the sword comes and takes his life, his blood will be on his own head.

Since he heard the sound of the trumpet but did not take warning, his blood will be on his own head. If he had taken warning, he would have saved himself.

But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not blow the trumpet to warn the people and the sword comes and takes the life of one of them, that man will be taken away because of his sin, but I will hold the watchman accountable for his blood.'

Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel; so hear the word I speak and give them warning from Me. When I say to the wicked, 'O wicked man, you will surely die', and you do not speak to dissuade him from his ways, that wicked man will die for his sin, and I will hold you accountable for his blood. But if you do warn the wicked man to turn from his ways and he does not do so, he will die for his sin, but you will have saved yourself. Son of man, say to the house of Israel, 'This is what you are saying:

C "Our offenses and sins weigh us down,

and we are wasting away because of them.

How then can we live?"'

M Say to them, 'As surely as I live, declares the sovereign LORD,

I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked,

but rather that they turn from their ways and live.

Turn! Turn from your evil ways!

Why will you die, O house of Israel?'"[^8]

The congregation sings (Psalm 130 or selected verses from Psalm 51):

M "Christ is the end of the law

so that there may be righteousness

for everyone who believes.

Moses describes in this way

the righteousness that is by the law:

'The man who does these things will live by them.'

But the righteousness that is by faith says:

'Do not say in your heart,

Who will ascend into heaven?

(that is, to bring Christ down)

or Who will descend into the deep?'

(that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).

But what does it say?

'The word is near you;

it is in your mouth and in your heart,'

that is the word of faith we are proclaiming:

that if you confess with your mouth, 'Jesus is Lord',

and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead,

you will be saved.

C For it is with our heart that we believe and are justified,

and it is with our mouth that we confess and are saved.

M As the Scripture says, "Anyone who trusts in Him

will never be put to shame."

C For there is no difference between Jew and Gentile —

the same Lord is Lord of all

and richly blesses all who call on Him, for

"everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved."

M How then, can they call on the one they have not believed in?

And how can they believe in the one of whom they have not heard?

And how can they hear without someone preaching to them?

And how can they preach unless they are sent?

As it is written:

C "How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!"[^9]

M Faith comes from hearing the message and the message is heard

through the word of Christ."[^10]

A psalm or hymn is sung expressing the joy of the gospel.

M "Jesus said to Simon Peter, 'Simon, son of John,

do you truly love Me more than these?'

He said,

Minister to be ordained / installed:

'Yes Lord, You know that I love You.'

M Jesus said, 'Feed My lambs'.

Again Jesus said,

'Simon, son of John, do you truly love Me?'

And he answered,

Minister to be ordained / installed:

'Yes, Lord, you know that I love You.'

M Jesus said, 'Take care of My sheep.'

M The third time He said... 'Simon, son of John,

do you love Me?'...

He said,

Minister to be ordained / installed:

'Lord, You know all things, You know that I love You.'

M 'Feed My sheep...Follow Me'."[^11]

A psalm or hymn is to be sung, expressing love for the Lord.

Then follows a prayer for which the following words may be used or in which the minister, using his own words, expresses the following thoughts:

Almighty God and loving Father in our Lord Jesus Christ,

We offer unto You thanksgiving and praise for having made known to us the gospel of Jesus Your Son. When we were dead in transgressions and sins He died our death and took away for ever the burden of Your holy anger. When He had finished bearing our punishment and paying the price for our redemption You raised Him up to a life that all who believe in Him will share for ever.

We praise You for the Word made flesh and for the Scriptures which tell us about Him, shining as a light before our feet until the great morning will dawn when our faith shall be sight and our pilgrimage will be over. We bless You and thank You for the Holy Spirit guiding Your people in all Your truth.

We thank You also, that, whereas Your whole Church is called to be the bearer of this good news in the world, You have given to her pastors and teachers to equip the saints for this ministry, to open Your Word to us and show us Your way of repentance, faith and joyful obedience.

We pray that You will guide Your servants with Your holy Spirit, that they will humbly submit to the truth You have revealed, making their own thoughts captive to the obedience of Your Word. Encourage them with Your promises and make them bold with Your authority. Clothe them with holiness of life and give them compassion that they may reach out to those wandering as sheep without a shepherd.

Here special points for intercession may be made, including the family of the ordinand.

Now open our hearts to Your Word that we may receive it in humility and joy and be doers of that Word in our daily lives.

For Jesus' sake,

C Amen.

THE MINISTRY OF THE WORD

Scripture reading(s).

Text and sermon.

A psalm or hymn is sung.

THE ORDINATION / INSTALLATION[^12]

M Congregation of Jesus Christ: Your Session has previously made known

to you the name of our brother       Full Name       called to the ministry of the Word and sacraments in this church. No one has brought forward anything lawful against his person or teaching. We shall therefore proceed, in the name of the Lord, to his ordination/installation.

Let us first call to mind what God's Word teaches concerning this office within the church.

All members of the Body of Christ are office-bearers, called to be prophets,[^13] priests and kings unto God,[^14] needing no mediators between God and man apart from Jesus Christ.[^15]

Christ, however, gathering His church on earth, gave her as His gifts special office bearers, pastors and teachers, to prepare God's people for works of service.[^16] Servants they are of God and of His people, following in the footsteps of Him who came not to be served but to serve, giving His life as a Ransom for many.[^17] Servants they are of their risen Lord who said, "Whoever accepts anyone I send, accepts Me; and whoever accepts Me, accepts the One who sent Me."[^18]

The first and foremost call of the minister of the Word is to preach the gospel of the kingdom,[^19] calling sinners to be reconciled to God,[^20] and nurturing believers in the faith and life of His kingdom.[^21]

Humble and diligent daily study of God's Word will enable the servant of the gospel to proclaim and apply it in the public worship of the church, in the teaching of its youth, in the homes of its members and in confrontation with those outside. Feeding eagerly upon the Word he can feed others, refute errors, warn the wayward, comfort the grieving, and lead God's people into a joyful life of hope and obedience.

C "Do good to Your servants and we will live;

we will obey Your Word.

Open our eyes that we may see wonderful things in Your law."[^22]

M The minister of the Word is also called to administer the sacraments which Christ has given as signs and seals of His grace. Christ said, "Make disciples of all nations, baptising them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"[^23] and, speaking of the Lord's Supper, "Do this in remembrance of Me."[^24]

C "Christ is the Mediator of a new covenant

that we who are called may receive

the promised eternal inheritance."[^25]

M The minister of the Word is also called to give leadership in the service of prayer. Following his Master and the apostles, he will bring the needs of God's people before the throne of grace[^26] and ensure that, in public worship, "requests, prayers, intercession and thanksgiving be made for everyone — for kings and all those in authority,"[^27] for the needs of God's people and for those in the world around them.[^28]

C "May our prayer be set before You like incense;

may the lifting up of our hands be like the evening sacrifice."[^29]

M Together with the elders, the minister of the Word is called to shepherd the people of God in the Christian life, giving guidance and counsel, keeping the church of God in good order and discipline. Both in encouragement and rebuke they are to make faithful use of the keys of the kingdom, binding on earth what shall be bound in heaven, and loosing on earth what shall be loosed in heaven.[^30]

C "The LORD is God! It is He who made us, and we are His.

We are His people, the sheep of His pasture."[^31]

M And now, brother       Name      , in order that it may be clear to us all that you are ready to accept this office, will you please stand and answer the following questions before God and His people.

The ordinand stands.

1. Do you believe in your heart that, in the call of this congregation it is the Lord Jesus Christ Himself who called you to be His servant, minister of His gospel?

2. Do you believe the Scriptures of the Old and New Testament to be the Word of God, the only infallible rule of faith and practice, and do you accept the doctrinal standards of the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia as being in harmony with these Scriptures?

Do you promise to proclaim and defend this gospel, and to reject all teachings that contradict it; to speak against all injustice and all untruth, and model for this congregation God's concern for compassion, justice and peace?

3. Do you promise to carry out the duties of your office faithfully, to love the church and all its members, and to live a life that is worthy of your calling?

4. Do you promise to submit to the government and discipline of the church, when it calls you to the obedience of the Word of God?

The ordinand answers:

By the grace of God, with all my heart, I do.

If this is an ordination, the ordinand shall now kneel. All ministers present and such elders as are appointed to do so by Session, shall lay their right hand on his head.

If this is an installation of a minister already ordained before, the installing minister shall lift his hand in the gesture of blessing.

M God our heavenly Father, who has called you to this holy office, enlighten you with His Spirit, strengthen you with His hand, and so govern and use you in your ministry, that it shall bear rich fruit both for this life and for the life to come, to the glory of His name and the coming of the kingdom of His Son Jesus Christ.

C Amen.

The newly ordained minister shall remain standing and face the congregation. The officiating minister shall ask the congregation to arise and will address them:

M People of God, members of this congregation,

do you receive this servant of your Lord and Redeemer from His hand, do you promise to receive the Word of truth from his lips in love and obedience, to remember him in prayer and encourage him in his work? Do you promise to work together with him and the other office bearers in making known the gospel of Christ?

C By the grace God, with all our heart, we do.

Here a psalm (suggested: Psalm 134) or hymn shall be sung. Then all shall be seated. The officiating minister or another appointed by Session shall present the charges:

CHARGES

M Dear brother and fellow-servant in Christ.

Keep watch over yourself and all the flock of which the Holy Spirit has made you a guardian. Be a shepherd of the church of your Lord, which He bought with His own blood.[^32]

Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a workman who does not need to be ashamed and who correctly handles the Word of truth.[^33]

Do not be domineering over those entrusted to you, but rather, to the believers and to all people, be an example[^34] in speech and life, in love, in faith and in purity.[^35]

Do not be ashamed of testifying about our Lord[^36] but by the power of God who saved us and called us to a holy life, take your share of suffering for the sake of the gospel.[^37] Then, when the Chief Shepherd appears, you too will receive the crown of glory that will never fade away.[^38]

And you, congregation of our Lord Jesus, receive your minister in the Lord with all joy and hold him in honour.[^39] When he preaches to you from the Holy Scriptures, accept it not as the word of man, but as it actually is, the Word of God.[^40]

Then the peace of God shall enter your lives and your homes, and believing in Christ, you shall inherit eternal life.

No man can do these things in his own strength. Let us therefore call upon the name of God:

Merciful Father, we thank You that Your Son, our Lord Jesus, through the service of men, gathers His Church from a lost human race unto life eternal. We thank You for this servant of Yours, sent to Your people as a messenger of Your peace.

Fill him mightily with Your Holy Spirit. Enlighten his mind to know the truth of Your Word. Give him the words so that he may fearlessly make known the mystery of the gospel.[^41]

Endow him with wisdom to give care and guidance to the people among whom You have placed him. Through his ministry, preserve Your church in peace, and make her grow in love, hope and joyful witness to those around her.

Give Your servant courage to face hardships, and compassion in the face of sorrow, both in others and in himself. Make him always willing to repent of evil and — with courage — to fight the devil both in his own life and in the lives of those entrusted to him.

Give him the joy of Your salvation, to be his strength until he, with all those who love Christ's appearance, shall see Him face to face.

This we ask in the name of the Lord Jesus, who taught us to say:

C Our Father in heaven,

hallowed be Your name,

Your kingdom come,

Your will be done on earth as in heaven.

Give us today our daily bread.

Forgive us our sins, as we forgive those who sin against us.

Lead us not into temptation but deliver us from the evil one.

For the kingdom, the power, and the glory are yours

now and for ever. Amen.

It may be appropriate to have the newly ordained / installed minister sign the Form of Subscription in the service. This Form should then first be read out for the information of the congregation, and may be found on page 185.

The offering is taken, a psalm or hymn is sung and the service ends with the benediction.

[^1]: It is assumed that the service is led by a minister (or ministers) other than the one who is to be ordained/installed, except for the final benediction which, traditionally, is often given by the new minister. Furthermore, there may be circumstances in which the preaching of the inaugural sermon by the newly ordained/installed minister is to take place in the same service in which he is ordained/installed. The point at which this is to take place would be after the "charges". Even if both ministers keep their sermon short, it makes for a long service, however. It is suggested that the two men involved cooperate in such a way that the two sermons, as much as possible, form a unity.This form is to be used flexibly. As with other participatory forms, one of the main aims is to spread the teaching element so that it can be more easily followed and absorbed by the congregation. Rather than using this form for a whole service, Sessions may also begin using it from page 285, "The Ordination/Installation."
