# Form / Guidelines For Funerals

INTRODUCTORY REMARKS

Funerals are the responsibility of the relatives or friends of the deceased, but the church welcomes the opportunity to minister God's Word to the living (Church Order, Article 71), to bring comfort to the bereaved when a loved one has died as a child of God, and/or to challenge the unbelieving with the gospel of Christ.

The choice of a text and the preaching will have to be made relevant to the circumstances. There may be joy and thanksgiving because the Lord has ended the suffering on earth of one of His dear children and has taken him / her into His glory, or there may be deep sorrow because of what seems an untimely and tragic death.

Sometime during the service in the church or at the cemetery, it may be appropriate to give expression of thanks to God for His blessings and the triumph of His grace through the person and life of the deceased, or such personal comments can be incorporated into the preaching of the Word. A very suitable time for this would be the prayer after the sermon.

The following is only a suggested model. The service in the church can either precede or follow the actual interment. The prayers are a guide only; words of a personal nature and expressions of thanks and praise can be used wherever applicable.

SERVICE IN THE CHURCH, CHAPEL, OR HOME

Call to Worship

Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted.[^1] For as in Adam all die, so in Christ will all be made alive.[^2]

Other suitable texts:

Deuteronomy 33:27; Job 1:21b; 19:25-27.

Psalm 30:5b; 103:13-14; John 11:25-26.

2 Corinthians 1:3-4.

Silent Prayer

Let each of us now in a moment of silence seek the blessing and nearness of God upon us so that His grace may strengthen us.

Votum and Salutation

Our help is in the name of the Lord, the Maker of heaven and earth.[^3] Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.[^4]

We welcome you to this worship service on the occasion of the funeral of Full Name and we thank you for joining the relatives in this time of grief in which we may look to the salvation riches of Christ.

Singing

(It will be convenient for those present, and especially at the grave-side, to have the words of the songs on printed sheets.)

Prayer

Father of mercies, God of all comfort.

Look upon us in Your tender love and compassion. Strengthen us all in this time of grief, especially Husband / Wife / Children / or whoever are the ones most afflicted by bereavement . May Your comforting grace in Christ Jesus be with those who have lost a loved one in death. May we see You in faith and know You as our Refuge and Strength, an ever-present help in trouble.

May Your peace be upon us, the peace that transcends all understanding, which will guard our hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.[^5] Let Your Word be a lamp to our feet and a guide for our path[^6] through the working of Your Holy Spirit.

Help us see Christ who in His death and resurrection won life out of death[^7] and who is to us the Way, the Truth and the Life.[^8]

Amen.

Scripture Reading(s) and Sermon

Many Scripture passages are suitable, for example:

Psalm 23; 39; 73; 90; 103; 121; 139.

John 11; 14.

Romans 8.

1 Corinthians 15; 2 Corinthians 4; 5.

1 Thessalonians 5.

Revelation 7; 21; 22.

Prayer[^9]

Eternal God, our heavenly Father.

You love us with an everlasting love and You turn away the darkness of the shadow of death.[^10] You have sent our Saviour Jesus Christ to bring life and immortality to light.[^11] We thank You that by His death You have destroyed the power of death, and that by His glorious resurrection You have opened the kingdom of heaven to all who believe.[^12]

Grant us the assurance that because Jesus lives we shall live also, and that neither death nor life, neither the present nor the future, shall separate us from Your love that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.[^13]

Help us now to wait on You with reverent and submissive hearts as we journey to the grave-side.[^14] As we gather there for the burial, we pray that You will remind us of the eternal values and that through patience and the encouragement of Your Word we may have hope.[^15]

Lift us up above our sorrow and distress into the light and peace of Your presence,[^16] through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Amen.

The Committal

EITHER:

Seeing that it has pleased Almighty God, in His wise providence, to take to Himself our brother / our sister / this child Name , we therefore commit his / her body to the ground,[^20] looking for the resurrection of the dead and the life of the world to come, through our Lord Jesus Christ, at whose coming in glorious majesty, the earth and the sea shall give up their dead[^21] and the mortal bodies of those who sleep in Him shall be changed and made like unto His own glorious body, according to His mighty power whereby He is able to bring everything under His control.[^22]

"Then I heard a voice from heaven say, 'Write: Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on.' 'Yes', says the Spirit, 'they will rest from their labour, for their deeds will follow them.'"[^23]

OR:

Since it has pleased our sovereign Lord to call into His glory our beloved brother / sister / this child Name , we now commit his/her body to the ground,[^24] in the sure and certain hope of the resurrection to eternal life in never-ending joy and beauty.

"Then I heard a voice from heaven say, 'Write: Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on.' 'Yes', says the Spirit, 'they will rest from their labour, for their deeds will follow them'."[^25]

OR:

Seeing that the earthly life of Name has come to an end, we therefore commit his/her body to the ground,[^26] earth to earth, dust to dust, until the day of the general resurrection of all people, when Christ will come to judge the living and the dead.

"Come unto Me, all you who are weary and burdened," says Jesus, "and I will give you rest."[^27]

Lord's Day 1, from the Heidelberg Catechism[^28]

What is your only comfort in life and in death?

That I am not my own,

but belong —

body and soul,

in life and in death —

to my faithful Saviour Jesus Christ.

He has fully paid for all my sins with His precious blood,

and has set me free from the tyranny of the devil.

He also watches over me in such a way

that not a hair can fall from my head

without the will of my Father in heaven:

in fact, all things must work together for my salvation.

Because I belong to Him,

Christ, by His Holy Spirit,

assures me of eternal life

and makes me wholeheartedly willing and ready

from now on to live for Him.

Prayer

O God of infinite compassion, You are the Comforter of your children in Jesus Christ. Look down in tender love and compassion upon Your grieving children on whom this trial has come. In the stillness of our hearts we plead for them Your sustaining grace.

Lord, will You be their strength and shield, that, trusting in You to lighten their burden, they may know Your presence to be near, and in the assurance of Your love, be delivered from their distress.

Hear us through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
