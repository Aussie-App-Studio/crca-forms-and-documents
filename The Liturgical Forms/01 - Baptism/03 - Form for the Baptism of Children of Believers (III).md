# Form for the Baptism of Children of Believers (III)

— a baptismal service —

CALL TO WORSHIP AND OPENING OF SERVICE

M[^1] O give thanks to the LORD, call on His name,

make known among the nations what He has done.

C He remembers His covenant for ever,

the word He commanded for a thousand generations.

M Glory in His holy name,

let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.

C Praise the LORD![^2]

Optional: silent prayer.

M Our help is in the name of the LORD

C Who made heaven and earth.

M Grace to you and peace from God our Father

and from our Lord Jesus Christ,

in the fellowship of the Holy Spirit.

C Amen.

A psalm or hymn of praise is sung.

M Our risen Lord Jesus said to His followers, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me. Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptising them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely, I will be with you always, to the very end of the age."[^3] By giving us this sign of being washed with water our Lord tells us that we are unclean before God. Our sins need to be taken away if we are to enter the kingdom of God.

Let us now come before the Lord in prayer and humbly confess our sins.

C Almighty and most merciful Father,

we have strayed from Your ways like lost sheep.

We have left undone what we ought to have done,

and we have done what we ought not to have done.

We have followed our own ways and the desires of our own hearts.

We have broken Your holy laws in our thoughts, words and deeds.

Through our sins we deserve Your condemnation.

O Lord God, Lamb of God, Son of the Father,

Who takes away the sin of the world,

have mercy on us and hear our prayer.

Amen.

M People of God, in our baptism we are assured that the blood of Jesus Christ, God's Son, cleanses us of all sin, and that His Spirit renews us from day to day.

We and our children are called back to the triune God in a restored covenant relationship. For when we are baptised into the name of the Father, God the Father assures us that He adopts us as His children and surrounds us with His love and care even in times of testing and trial.

C God, our Father and Creator, we praise and thank You that You gave us life and adopted us as Your children.

M When we are baptised into the name of the Son, God the Son assures us that through His death and resurrection we are set free from the condemnation of sin, and are made right with God by faith in Him alone.

C Son of God, Saviour, we praise and thank You for Your sacrifice and victory.

M When we are baptised into the name of the Holy Spirit, God the Holy Spirit assures us that He, the Giver of faith and new life, will live within us and change us by His power from day to day until at last we shall be presented spotless before God in the full splendour of life eternal.

C Holy Spirit, our Guide and Companion, we praise and thank You for the life and hope You give us!

M So God, who promises us all these things, calls us in this sacrament to begin living that new life in purity and holiness. He made a covenant with us: "I will be your God, you will be My people."[^4]

He calls us to love Him with all our heart, soul, mind and strength. He calls us to be on His side in the battle against Satan and his works, both within ourselves and in the world around us. Jesus calls us to carry our cross in His footsteps and accept suffering as living members of His body.

If then through weakness we fall into sin, it is He who calls us never to doubt His mercy nor to remain in sin or give ourselves up to despair. We are to remember joyfully that God's covenant with us is for ever, and so with His help we can overcome and are encouraged to go on in His service.

C Sing BoW 474:3, or similar hymn or psalm:

I heard the voice of Jesus say,

'I am this dark world's Light;

look unto Me, your morn shall rise

and all your days be bright!'

I came to Jesus and I found

in Him my Star, my Sun;

and in that Light of life I'll walk

till travelling days are done.

M Scripture calls Abraham the father of all believers. He was given the covenant sign of circumcision after he came to believe in God.[^5] However, God also commanded Abraham that this same sign of His grace be given to his small children.[^6] The grace of God is still the same today and includes our children in His covenant people. Scripture calls the children of believers holy[^7] and God's promises are for them as well as for us. God does not only call individuals but a people to be His very own.[^8] It is His will that all nations are to become His disciples.[^9]

Christian parents are entrusted with the vital task of bringing up their children in the Lord. They, with the help of God's people around them, are to lead them to faith and conversion by instruction in the holy ways of God, by prayer and the example of Christian living.

Let us pray.

We praise You, almighty Father, for saving the family of faith carried in the ark when the waters of the flood covered a rebellious world.[^10]
We praise You, for setting Your people free as You led them safely through the waters of the Red Sea that swallowed up the oppressor.[^11]

C Father, You are our God, we are Your people.

M We praise You, Christ our Lord, for going down into the waters of Jordan and coming up to receive the Spirit; for sinking into the depth of our eternal death, to be raised up Lord of our life!

C Christ Jesus, Son of God, You are our Shepherd, we are the sheep of Your pasture!

M We praise You, Holy Spirit, for dwelling in us. We pray that You will separate us from sin and openly mark us with a faith that can stand the light of day and endure the dark of night.

C Spirit of God, keep prompting us to glad obedience.

M We ask You now for Name of Child that he/she may grow in grace and the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ, may learn what it means to walk joyfully in the footsteps of his/her Master, cheerfully enduring testing, trial and scorn of the godless, until at the end when God recreates the heaven and the earth he/she will see his/her Lord's face in never ending delight.

C Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, You are our God, we are Your people. Amen.

The minister requests the parents to stand and says, addressing them by name:

Names of Parents , since you have presented Name of Child for holy baptism, you are asked to:

(EITHER):

answer the following questions before God and His people:

1. Do you confess Jesus Christ as your Lord and Saviour, accept the promises of God, and affirm the truth of the Christian faith which is taught in the Bible and confessed in this church of Christ?

2. Do you believe that your child, though sinful by nature, is received by God in Christ as a member of His covenant and His church, and therefore ought to be baptised?

3. Do you promise, in reliance on the Holy Spirit and with the help of the Christian community, to do all in your power to instruct this child in the truth of the Christian faith and to lead him/her by your example to confess and follow Jesus Christ as his/her own Lord and Saviour?

Answer:

By the grace of God, we do.

(OR):

make your baptismal vow before God and His people:

(The parents say, if possible in unison)

"We confess Jesus Christ as our Lord and Saviour, accept the promises of God, and affirm the truth of the Christian faith which is proclaimed in the Bible and confessed in the articles of our Christian faith as taught in this church of Jesus Christ.

We believe that our child, Full Name though sinful by nature right from the beginning of his/her life, and consequently deserving of God's judgment and even eternal death is by God's mercy, holy in Christ and therefore, as a member of Christ's church, ought to be baptised.

We realise that this child will have to confess Christ as his/her own Saviour. And so we promise, in reliance on the Holy Spirit and with the help of the Christian community, to do all in our power to instruct Full Name in the Christian faith, cause him/her to be so instructed, and to lead him/her by our example into a life of Christian discipleship."

The minister says:

Jesus said, "Let the little children come to Me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these."[^12]

In administering baptism, the minister shall say:

Full Name  , I baptise you into the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

The minister invites the congregation to stand, and addresses them as follows:

Do you, the people of the Lord, promise to receive Name of Child in love, pray for him/her and help care for his/her instruction in the faith? Do you promise so to order your life and witness as a congregation that he/she may grow up in the knowledge and love of God, and be surrounded by Christian example and influence?

Answer: By the grace of God, we do.

A psalm or hymn is sung.

The minister leads in the baptismal prayer:

Lord our God, forever faithful to Your promise, we thank You for again assuring us and our children that You have forgiven us and received us as children in Christ.

Give wisdom and love to     Names of Parents     and to all of us as we carry out the vows just made.  We pray that You will guide our children throughout their lives, and enable them to respond in faith to the gospel.  Fill them with Your Spirit and make their lives fruitful.  Uphold them in temptation, struggle and pain, and when Jesus returns, let them celebrate forever with all the people of God Your greatness and goodness, in the joy of Your new creation.

For Jesus' sake. Amen.

The service continues with the ministry of the Word.
