# Form For The Preparation
Of The Lord's Supper (II)

Beloved in the Lord, as we hope to celebrate the Lord's Supper coming Sunday, it is our duty to prepare ourselves, as Paul says: "A man ought to examine himself before he eats of the bread and drinks of the cup."[^1]

In this self-examination each of us is faced with these questions:

Do I believe that my sins are forgiven only because my Lord sacrificed Himself for me on the cross?

Is it my sincere desire to love God and my neighbour, and to serve them according to God's Word?

All those who, in answer to these questions, seek their refuge in Jesus Christ alone, are invited by the Lord to come with joyful praise to His table.

Those who live in unbelief and sin will have to repent and seek forgiveness through our Lord Jesus Christ, otherwise they have no right to come to the table of the Lord, nor do they have part in the kingdom of God.

Let us pray:

Almighty God, we pray for Your grace that in the coming days we may rightly examine ourselves. Comfort us as repentant sinners and give us the assurance that we may look forward to a blessed celebration. And if any of us are not right with You, give grace and mercy, Lord, that in repentance they may change their way of life, and then join us coming Sunday in the celebration of this most glorious feast.

Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

---

[^1]: 1 Corinthians 11:28
