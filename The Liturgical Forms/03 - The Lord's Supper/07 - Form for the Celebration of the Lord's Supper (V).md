# Form For The Celebration
Of The Lord's Supper (V)

M[^1] The Lord be with you

C[^2] The Lord be with you also.

M Lift up your hearts!

C We lift them up to the Lord!

M Let us give thanks to the Lord.

C He is worthy to be praised.

M Truly it is fitting, it is our joy and happiness to give thanks to You, almighty Father, eternal God, through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

He took the form of man. He humbled Himself to death, even the death of the cross.

Therefore, You have highly exalted Him and given Him the name that is above every name, that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth, to the glory of God the Father.[^3]

Therefore, with the glorified Church and all the company of heaven, we praise You, as we sing with joy:

C Holy, holy, holy! Lord God almighty!

all Thy works shall praise Thy name,

in earth and sky and sea;

holy, holy, holy! Merciful and mighty,

God in three Persons,

blessed Trinity![^4]

THE INSTITUTION

M In the night in which He was betrayed, our Lord Jesus took bread, gave thanks, broke it and said, "This is My body, which is for you. Do this in remembrance of Me."

In the same way, after supper, He took the cup, saying,

"This cup is the new covenant in My blood,

do this, whenever you drink it, in remembrance of Me."[^5]

C So we remember

the redeeming sufferings of the Messiah,

our Lord, who rose again and lives.

We will proclaim His death till He comes.

Maranatha![^6]

M Let us pray.

Prayer[^7]

Merciful God and Father,

we humbly ask You, that in this Supper,

in which we cherish the blessed memory

of the bitter death of Your Son, Jesus Christ,

You will so work in our hearts through the Holy Spirit,

that we, with true confidence,

surrender more and more to Your Son Jesus Christ,

in order that our burdened and contrite hearts,

through the power of the Holy Spirit,

may be nourished and refreshed with His true body and blood,

yes, with Him who is truly God and man, the only heavenly bread;

and that we may no longer live in our sins,

but He in us and we in Him,

and so truly be partakers of the new and everlasting testament,

the covenant of grace,

that we do not doubt that You will forever be our gracious Father,

never again holding our sins against us,

and providing us as Your dear children and heirs

with all we need for body and soul.

Hear us, O God and merciful Father, through Jesus Christ,

in whose name we pray with all Your children together:

C Our Father in heaven,

hallowed be Your name,

Your Kingdom come,

Your will be done, on earth as in heaven.

Give us today our daily bread.

Forgive us our sins, as we forgive those who sin against us.

Lead us not into temptation but deliver us from the evil one.

For the kingdom, the power and the glory are Yours,

now and for ever.

Amen.

AGNUS DEI (Lamb of God)

All sing:

O Lamb of God most holy!

Who on the cross did languish;

e'er patient, meek and lowly,

though mocked amid Your anguish;

all sin You have borne for us,

else would despair reign o'er us;

have mercy on us, O Jesus!

Your peace be with us, O Jesus![^8]

THE INVITATION

The following invitation is for communicant members and guests known to Session. This should have been made plain in the church bulletin or by way of announcement before the service, so that the words of the invitation are not misunderstood.

The naming of the guests can take place at the point indicated below by the asterisk (*).

M Congregation in the Lord Jesus Christ, the Lord has prepared His table for all who love Him and trust in Him alone for their salvation. All who are truly sorry for their sins, who sincerely believe in the Lord Jesus as their Saviour, and who desire to live in obedience to Him, are now invited to come with gladness to the table of the Lord. *

All those, however, who do not have this faith and repentance in their hearts, but who live in wilful disobedience to the Lord, are urged to turn from these ways of rebellion, or — if not — to stay away from this food and drink which Christ has ordained for His people, lest they eat and drink judgment on themselves.[^9]

Come then, for all things are now ready.

THE COMMUNION

When the people are ready to eat the bread:

Take, eat, remember and believe that the body of our Lord Jesus Christ was given for a complete forgiveness of all our sins.

When the people are ready to drink from the cup:

Take, drink, remember and believe that the precious blood of our Lord Jesus Christ was given for a complete forgiveness of all our sins.

THE THANKSGIVING

Congregation in Christ,

since the Lord has fed us at His table, let us praise His holy name with thanksgiving.

C Praise the LORD, O my soul;

all my inmost being, praise His holy name.

Praise the LORD, O my soul,

and forget not all His benefits —

who forgives all your sins

and heals all your diseases,

who redeems your life from the pit

and crowns you with love and compassion,

who satisfies your desires with good things!...

For as high as the heavens are above the earth,

so great is His love for those who fear Him;

as far as the east is from the west,

so far has He removed our transgressions from us.

As a father has compassion on his children,

so the LORD has compassion on those who fear Him.

Praise the LORD, O my soul![^10]

Therefore shall my mouth and heart

show forth the praise of the LORD,

from this time onward for evermore.

Amen.

A psalm or hymn may be sung.

---

[^1]: M = Minister
[^2]: C = Congregation, in unison
[^3]: Philippians 2:7-11
[^4]: Book of Worship hymn 164:4, to be sung without prelude
[^5]: 1 Corinthians 11:23-26
[^6]: The word "Maranatha!" which occurs in older translations of 1 Corinthians 16:22, can mean three things: "The Lord has come", "The Lord is coming", and the prayer we find in the NIV: "O Lord, come!".
[^7]: This prayer is based on that of the classic Form which, originating from Calvin, received its basic shape in the Palatinate, where the Heidelberg Catechism was written. Olevianus was also one of the authors of this Form. It has been used in Reformed churches all over the world for centuries.
[^8]: Book of Worship hymn 298:1, to be sung unannounced, without prelude
[^9]: 1 Corinthians 11:27-29
[^10]: Psalm 103:1-5a; 11-13; 22b
