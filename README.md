# CRCA Forms & Documents

A free, easy-to-use browser add-on that puts all **Christian Reformed Churches of Australia (CRCA)** liturgical forms, creeds, confessions, Church Order, and worship resources right at your fingertips — even without an internet connection.

Built for pastors, elders, worship leaders, church administrators, and church members.

## How to Add It to Your Browser

Because this tool is shared directly for free rather than through the Chrome Web Store, setup takes about 2 minutes:

### 1. Unzip the downloaded file
- **Windows:** Open your **Downloads** folder, right-click **`CRCA-Forms-Extension.zip`**, click **Extract All…**, and click **Extract**.
- **Mac:** Open your **Downloads** folder and **double-click** **`CRCA-Forms-Extension.zip`**.

This creates a regular folder called **`CRCA-Forms-Extension`**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Keep this folder!** Move **`CRCA-Forms-Extension`** somewhere safe (like your **Documents** folder) and don't delete it — your browser loads the add-on from this folder. You can safely delete the `.zip` file afterwards.

### 2. Open your browser's Extensions page
Open a new tab, type the address for your browser into the address bar at the top, and press **Enter**:

| Browser | Type into the address bar |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Chrome** | `chrome://extensions` |
| **Microsoft Edge** | `edge://extensions` |
| **Brave** | `brave://extensions` |

### 3. Turn on "Developer mode"
Find the **Developer mode** switch on that page and turn it **ON**:
- **Chrome / Brave:** top-right corner
- **Microsoft Edge:** left side panel

*(Don't worry about the name — it's just the setting that lets your browser load a free add-on from your computer instead of the store.)*

### 4. Add the folder to your browser
1. Click the **Load unpacked** button (near the top-left).
2. Choose the **`CRCA-Forms-Extension`** folder you unzipped in Step 1 (select the **folder itself**, not the `.zip` file).
3. Click **Select Folder** (Windows) or **Open** (Mac).

**Done!** **CRCA Forms & Documents** is now added to your browser.

### 5. Pin it to your toolbar (Optional, Recommended)
1. Click the **puzzle piece (🧩)** icon near the top-right of your browser.
2. Find **CRCA Forms & Documents** and click the **pin (📌)** icon (or **Show in toolbar** in Edge) so the CRCA icon is always one click away.

## What's Included

- **Ecumenical Creeds:** The Apostles' Creed, The Nicene Creed, and The Athanasian Creed
- **Reformed Confessions & Subscription:** The Belgic Confession, The Heidelberg Catechism, The Canons of Dort, The Westminster Confession of Faith, and the CRCA Form of Subscription
- **Contemporary Testimony:** Our World Belongs to God
- **The Liturgical Forms:** Baptism (Children I–IV & Adults), Public Profession of Faith, Lord's Supper (Preparation I–II & Celebration I–V), Church Discipline, Ordination & Installation of Office Bearers, Marriage, and Funerals
- **CRCA Church Order:** CRCA Church Order (2022) and Rules for the Final Examination (2024)
- **Acts of Synod:** The Acts of Synod 2024
- **Other Resources (Worship & Ministry Training):** Calls to Worship, Greetings & Benedictions, Readings of the Law, Assurances of Pardon, Readings at the Offerings, Alternatives & Options, and RTC Church Based Training (CBT)

## Helpful Features

- **Fast Search & Quick Jump:** Search by phrase or jump straight to `LD 1`, `Q 1`, `BC 14`, `CD 1:7`, `WCF 1:6`, or `CO 71`.
- **Service Prep & Option Selection:** Fill in names, pronouns, dates, and choose `EITHER / OR` options directly inside Liturgical Forms before copying or printing.
- **Church Order & Confessional Harmony:** Preview internal Church Order references (`Art 51`, `Art 23.f`) in place, jump between parallel Confessions, Church Order, and Liturgical Forms, or open **Side-by-Side Compare** in a full tab.
- **Read Your Way:** Open as a quick popup, in your browser's **Side Panel** alongside Google Docs or Word, in a full tab, or in distraction-free **Pulpit Mode**. Switch Light/Dark themes and adjust text size (`A-` / `A+`).
- **Clickable Footnotes & Scripture Links:** Tap any footnote number to preview references (linked to BibleGateway in your chosen translation), or hide footnotes with one click.
- **Rich-Text Copy, Slides & Word Export:** Copy rich + plain text for Word/Google Docs bulletins, copy single Q&As/sections or presentation **Slides**, download as **Word (`.doc`)**, or click **PDF / Print**.
- **Works Offline & Updates Automatically:** Cache all documents with one click (`Download All Offline`) for reliable offline use anywhere.
- **Bookmarks, Recents & Shortcuts:** Star frequently used forms, resume right where you left off, right-click selected text to search, or press `Alt+Shift+C` / `Alt+Shift+S`.

## Common Questions

**Is this safe? Why did I have to turn on "Developer mode"?**
Yes. It is completely free, has no ads or tracking, and only downloads the CRCA documents stored right here. "Developer mode" is simply the switch Chrome, Edge, and Brave require when adding an extension directly from a folder instead of the web store.

**My browser says "Manifest file is missing or unreadable."**
Make sure you **unzipped** `CRCA-Forms-Extension.zip` first, and that you selected the unzipped **`CRCA-Forms-Extension`** folder (not the `.zip` file).

**The add-on stopped working after I cleaned up my Downloads folder.**
Your browser reads the add-on directly from the unzipped folder. If that folder was moved or deleted, just unzip the file again into your **Documents** folder and repeat Step 4.

**How do I get updates?**
- **Document updates** arrive automatically — click the **Refresh (↻)** button at the top of the add-on to check for the latest text.
- **New add-on versions** can be installed by downloading the `.zip` file again, replacing the files in your **`CRCA-Forms-Extension`** folder, and clicking the reload icon on your browser's Extensions page.
