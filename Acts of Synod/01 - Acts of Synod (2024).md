## Article 7: Matters of Procedure

Synod decided on the following matters of procedure:

1. That, concerning the right of floor, the order will be similar to the previous Synod, and will be determined by the Chairman as appropriate. However, in general (and in keeping with the Rules for Procedure & Debate, adopted by Synod 1982, Art 29), delegates to the Synod shall have precedence over all other speakers, apart from a reporter for a committee who is not a delegate, and priority shall be given to those who have not already spoken. Advisors, fraternal delegates, and any office bearer of the CRCA who requests to be heard may be granted the floor by the Chairman as time and circumstances permit. No one shall speak longer than ten minutes, unless the synod, by a majority vote, permits him to extend his remarks.
2. To abide by the Rules of Debate, as found under “Appendix – Procedure & Debate” in the CRCA Decision Book.
3. To adopt the following time schedule:<br>Morning session: 9.00 am – 10.40 am / 11.00 am – 12.30 pm<br>Afternoon session: 2.00 pm – 3.40 pm / 4.00 pm – 5.30 pm<br>Evening session: 7.30 pm – 9.00 pm
4. To adopt the following time limits:
   1. that introduction of committee reports shall ordinarily be limited to five minutes;
   2. that, as a general rule, any other speech be limited to ten minutes;
   3. that addresses by Fraternal Delegates (and Observers, if applicable) be limited to ten minutes and our replies to five minutes.
5. That the reporters of all committees be required to organise recommendations contained in their Reports as follows:
   1. recommendations which do not require Synod to make changes or to do anything new are to be grouped together and voted on ‘in block’;
   2. recommendations which are likely to be passed unanimously by Synod are to be grouped together and voted on ‘in block’;
   3. remaining recommendations are to be voted on separately.
6. Re devotions:
   1. To set aside up to thirty minutes each morning for devotions, to be led by ministers from the Convening Church, RTC Faculty members and Rev Clinton Berends
   2. That other devotions be taken as per the Devotion Roster circulated at the start of Synod. The brothers leading these devotions are reminded to keep track of specific prayer requests made during the session preceding their turn on the roster.
7. That the checking and approval of the Minutes of the previous day’s sessions be done after morning tea each day.
8. To appoint the following press reporter:
   1. Rev Pieter Tuit for condensed news items (to be approved by the Moderamen) for uploading onto the CRCA Website.
9. Re the rate of reimbursement from synodical funds for travel on behalf of Synod and the denomination:
   1. that reimbursement for car travel during the next inter-synodical period be set at 50 cents per kilometre.
   2. that reimbursement for interstate travel during the next inter-synodical period be limited to the value of the discount air fare available at the time;
   3. that reimbursement for travel to and from this Synod is payable at the above rates to primary delegates, advisers, and those coming to Synod to report on the work of its deputies when the committee is not otherwise represented, with the limitation of 9.2, above, for interstate travel.
10. Regarding meals at Synod’s expense:
   1. that all meals be provided to all those who have registered to attend this Synod or part thereof.
   2. that casual visitors be requested to pay for meals at a rate of $10 for lunch and $15 for dinner.
11. To note that only the decisions of Synod and not the full discussion will be minuted by the Minute Clerk.
12. To appoint the following ad hoc committee:
   1. Financial Watchdog:
     - Troy Vander Noord plus
     - Herman Westendorp

## Article 14: Children and the Lord’s Supper - Overture

[Overture 6 - CRC Toowoomba, CRC Tivoli & Christian Reformation Community Church, Inala]<br>(Minutes Articles 14 & 39)

> **The following motions were lost:**

1. That a public profession of faith be required of all children prior to participation in the sacrament of Lord’s Supper.
2. That the qualifications for children coming to the Lord’s Supper table be amended as follows:
   - **i.** They must profess faith in Jesus and be baptised.
   - **ii.** There must be age-appropriate evidence of this faith being lived out.
   - **iii.** They must be able to articulate the content of the gospel as summarised in the Apostles Creed.
   - **iv.** They must be able to understand what it means to remember Christ’s death (v.25), proclaim Christ’s death (v.26), and to discern the body (v.29).
   - **v.** They must be capable of examining themselves without the assistance of an adult.
   - **vi.** They must understand that the bread represents Jesus’ body, and that the wine/juice represents His blood. They must understand that the supper is more than mere remembrance, but that they are spiritually nourished by Christ as they participate in faith at the table.
   - **vii.** They must be aware of, and understand, the dangers of participating unworthily in the supper.
3. That the Guidelines for Sessions who are assessing a child’s readiness to participate in the Lord’s Supper be adopted.
4. That we consult with the churches in Ecclesiastical Fellowship with the CRCA to ascertain the implications of our decisions on children at the Lord’s Supper made at Synod 2022 and Synod 2024.

> **The following recommendation was withdrawn:**

5. That a study committee be formed to consider the implications on how we work together as a denomination in view of our decisions on children at the Lord’s Supper.

> **Synod “laid on the table” the following recommendation:**

6. That each church has the freedom to supervise the table according to their own conscience and local practice. This means that if you are visiting or transferring to another CRCA church, you are required to submit to the local practice.

**[From Minutes Article 39: Children at the Lord’s Supper Ad Hoc Committee]**

**Synod decided:**

7. That each session/church council has the freedom to add additional qualifications (to those adopted by Synod 2022, Article 30, 4i-vi) when assessing the credibility of a child's faith for the purposes of determining if a child will be admitted to the Lord's Supper table. Those transferring into or visiting a church will have to submit to the local practice.

## Article 19: Human Resources Study Committee

[Report 8, Convenor – Chris Bongers]<br>(Minutes Article 19)

**Synod decided:**

1. To receive this report.
2. To direct the Business and Finance Committee (BFC), while establishing how best to financially and legally structure the overseas missions of the Christian Reformed Churches of Australia (CRCA), to also seek legal advice on:
   - **a.** The best company structure for employment purposes
   - **b.** the best model of membership for such a company or companies involving members of the BFC and Synodical Interim Committee (SIC)
   - **c.** any changes needed to the Church Order to reflect the legal structure of the company.
3. That once the company structure has been resolved, employment of staff be moved to the new company (Company).
4. That once the company structure has been resolved, all employees be placed on new contracts consistent with current employment standards, without change to existing additional benefits.
5. That each delegated subcommittee continue in their role as manager of the employee, following policies and procedures as set out by the Company.
6. That initially the BFC, and eventually the Company, in consultation with the Human Resources Study Committee (HRSC) make arrangements to either outsource the human resource function (excluding payroll and leave management), or seek a consultancy arrangement with, a likeminded organization with more human resource experience. This arrangement would be on the basis of a fee for service and would be available to the BFC, HRSC or Company and other committees or work groups to manage denominational employees and to the HRSC to develop systems, documentation and guidelines.
7. To make a budget allocation of $20,000 for the first year of engagement and $5,000 for subsequent years be made available to the BFC, HRSC or the Company for procuring these services. The additional funds in the first year is to establish consistency in current employment documents and working with committees and workgroups on implementation.
8. That a secure employment cloud environment be set up for long term storage of employee information and records, with access granted to the service provider, BFC or Company, relevant committees and CRCA employees.
9. To ask the HRSC to work with the Synod approved employer, workgroups and committees to establish:
   - **a.** a suitable job/position description format to be used for all denominational employees.
   - **b.** suitable guidelines for management of the human resources functions.
   - **c.** a suitable employee review system and templates.
10. To charge workgroups and committees to:
   - **a.** establish standardized job/position descriptions with current denominational employees, that accurately represent the tasks being undertaken by the employee.
   - **b.** ensure job/position descriptions clearly articulate and identify the employer and the reporting lines for employment purposes.
   - **c.** implement the guidelines into their practices of managing denominational employees and employment.
   - **d.** establish standardized review templates and procedures with committees and workgroups that employ denominational workers.
11. That current practices of Classis led employee reviews be changed to performance reviews by committees with denominational employees or by the HRSC.
12. That once the proper legal structure has been established, to charge the HRSC to work with the BFC or Company, committees and working groups to meet employer obligations.
13. To approve the establishment of human resource documents, policies and procedures to meet these obligations during the next inter-synodical period.
14. That the Human Resources Committee report on the progress of development and implementation of human resources functions at the 2027 Synod.

## Article 41: Next Synod

(Minutes Article 51)

Synod decided that Classis NSW be charged to convene the 2027 Synod. It was noted that the Wollongong CRC had offered to be the convening church subject to session approval. Synod agreed that an option would be to hold the Synod in a conference centre and noted that this had been budgeted for.

## Article 42: Vote of thanks

(Minutes Article 52)

At various stages during the last day of Synod 2024 the Chairman asked Synod to express thanks to those involved in the organising and running of Synod. This included the organising committee, those who worked so tirelessly in providing for our physical needs, our transport and especially from the kitchen and also those who provided billets for the many delegates, musicians and those helping out with sound and IT.

He also gave a vote of thanks for support provided by the Moderamen, Synodical Treasurer, Synodical Administrator, the delegates, advisers and fraternal delegates.

In response, on behalf of the delegates, the Vice-Chairman, Rev. Andrew Nugteren, thanked the Chairman for the efficient and competent way in which he had led this Synod.

## Article 43: Close of Synod

(Minutes Article 53)

The Chairman of Synod, Rev. John Zuidema gave the closing address and after prayer, declared the 2024 Synod closed at 5:30pm.
