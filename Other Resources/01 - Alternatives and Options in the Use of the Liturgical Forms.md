# Alternatives And Options
In The Use Of Liturgical Forms

Apart from using the forms on the occasions indicated, on the occasion of:

— public profession of faith by people not baptised in the Reformed Church(es):

use Form for Public Profession of Faith, page 216, modified;

— public profession of faith by baptised members as well as those baptised in other churches:

use Form for Public Profession of Faith, page 216, modified;

— public profession of faith and adult baptism:

use Form for the Baptism of the Children of Believers (II), page192, modified, with the questions from the Form for Public Profession of Faith, page 217;

— public profession of faith and adult baptism plus child baptism:

as above, but Baptism Form now not modified;

— public profession of faith of baptised members and adult baptism:

use Form for the Baptism of the Children of Believers (II), page 192, modified, then all the questions from the Form for Public Profession of Faith, page 217;

— public profession of faith of baptised members, adults, and child baptism:

as above, but Form for Baptism not modified; add to the questions for the profession of faith, questions 2 and 3 of the Form for the Baptism of the Children of Believers (II), page 194;

— preparation for the Lord's Supper:

use the first part of the Form for the Lord's Supper I, page 221;

— ordination / installation of ministers:

use the second part of the Form, beginning at page 285; most responses can be omitted if that is desired;

— ordination / installation of elders and deacons:

use the second part of the Form, beginning at page 295; most responses can be omitted if that is desired.
