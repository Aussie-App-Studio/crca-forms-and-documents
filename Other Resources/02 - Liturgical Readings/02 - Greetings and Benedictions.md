# Greetings and Benedictions

1. Romans 1:7

Grace and peace to you from God our Father

and from the Lord Jesus Christ.

2. Galatians 1:3-5

Grace and peace to you from God our Father

and the Lord Jesus Christ,

who gave Himself for our sins

to rescue us from the present evil age,

according to the will of our God and Father,

to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.

3. 1 Timothy 1:2

Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father

and Christ Jesus our Lord.

4. 2 Peter 1:2

Grace and peace be yours in abundance

through the knowledge of God

and of Jesus our Lord.

5. Revelation 1:4-6

Grace and peace to you from Him who is, and who was,

and who is to come,

and from the seven spirits before His throne,

and from Jesus Christ, who is the faithful witness,

the firstborn from the dead,

and the Ruler of the kings of the earth.

(To Him who loves us and has freed us

from our sins by His blood,

and has made us to be a kingdom and priests

to serve His God and Father —

to Him be glory and power for ever and ever!

Amen.)

6. Numbers 6:24-26

The LORD bless you and keep you;

the LORD make His face shine upon you

and be gracious to you;

the LORD turn His face toward you

and give you peace.

7. 2 Corinthians 13:14

May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ,

and the love of God,

and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all.

8. Romans 15:13

May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace

as you trust in Him,

so that you may overflow with hope

by the power of the Holy Spirit.

9. Philippians 4:7

The peace of God, which transcends all understanding,

will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

10. 1 Thessalonians 5:23-24

May God Himself, the God of peace,

sanctify you through and through.

May your whole spirit, soul and body be kept blameless

at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

The One who calls you is faithful and He will do it.

11. 2 Thessalonians 3:16

Now may the Lord of peace Himself give you peace

at all times and in every way.

The Lord be with all of you.
